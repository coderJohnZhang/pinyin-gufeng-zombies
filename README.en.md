# Pinyin & Gufeng Zombies

A tower-defense game for kids. Read pinyin and answer classical Chinese questions: correct answers fire peas and defeat zombies.

## How to Play

Open the link below in a browser on a computer or tablet to play. No installation is needed:

https://coderJohnZhang.github.io/pinyin-gufeng-zombies/

## Game Modes

- **Pinyin levels**: Tap a zombie to lock onto it, then tap the blue initial (声母) and the red final (韵母) to spell the sound shown above the zombie. Level 3 also requires choosing the tone.
- **Gufeng levels**: The line above a zombie is missing one character (shown as "□"). Choose the correct character from four options.
- Planting costs sun. Sunflowers produce sun, Peashooters shoot automatically, and Wall-nuts block zombies.
- Answer three questions correctly in a row to earn a "Combined Cannon", which clears the whole lawn.
- Finish a level to earn stars based on the lives you have left. Progress is saved in the current browser.

## Controls

| Device | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Mouse / touch | Tap a plant card to select it, then tap the lawn to plant. Tap a zombie to lock on. Tap cards to answer. |
| Keyboard | `Q` / `W` / `E` select plants; `1`–`4` choose options or tones; Space fires the Combined Cannon; `Esc` pauses. |

The Illustrated Guide (图鉴) shows examples of initials, finals, and tones, plus idiom meanings and classical poems. Tap a card to hear it read aloud, if the device has a Chinese voice installed.
